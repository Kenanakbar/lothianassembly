About us

Local, reliable & proud of our work

Lothian Assembly is an Edinburgh-based furniture assembly and handyman service. We take the stress out of flat pack furniture — from IKEA wardrobes and beds to office desks and shelving units, we handle it all quickly and professionally.

We also specialise in bathroom and kitchen silicone resealing, giving tired joints a fresh, clean finish. Every job — big or small — gets the same attention to detail and care.

Based in Edinburgh and serving all of The Lothians, we're your local, dependable team for getting things done properly.
